---
name: Crusade invitation privacy and attendance
description: Guest photo cards are private, self-declared invitations, not registrations.
---

Crusade invitation cards must not require an account or registration. Visitors' photos must not be permanently retained unless retention is necessary and explicitly disclosed.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a registration-free invitation generator with privacy, photo removal and replacement.

**How to apply:** Keep photo editing on the visitor's device. Do not turn the invitation flow into an attendee database or upload visitor photos as a convenience.

The requested “CONFIRMED & ATTENDING” badge represents the visitor's own stated intent, not a verified RSVP or reserved admission.

**Why:** The user wants the badge and the visual style of a registered-attendee reference, but expressly requires no registration.

**How to apply:** Preserve the requested badge, avoid “registered attendee” claims, and explain that generating a card does not create a registration.

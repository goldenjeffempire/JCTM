---
name: Devotion editorial policy
description: Non-repetition, preservation, AI approval, and authorship constraints for daily readings
---

Preserve valid published devotions. Never recycle an earlier topic or title to fill a date; include scheduled material when checking for repetition. Broad categories can recur, but their specific teaching and practical angle must be genuinely new.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested preservation and permanent non-repetition after the old finite pool repeatedly produced the same lessons.

**How to apply:** Keep publication fail-closed when no unused validated reading remains. Do not restore date-based rotating fallbacks or silently relax historical duplicate checks.

The user approved AI-assisted continuous expansion, including its external usage costs. Generated material must be biblically grounded and checked for originality and quality.

**Why:** The user selected AI-assisted expansion rather than editor-only additions.

**How to apply:** Preserve bounded background generation and independent review. Do not invent quotations from God or attribute newly authored material to Prophet Amos as if it were his own recorded speech. Provider outages must be surfaced to administrators, not concealed through recycled content.

Verify exact Scripture quotations against the named translation, including short excerpts; do not reconstruct quotations from memory.

**Why:** A spiritually accurate summary can still insert a word that is not present in a contiguous KJV excerpt.

**How to apply:** Check quotations independently before admitting them. Preserve published text except for the explicitly approved NKJV migration described below.

Use NKJV for all Bible passages, including previously published devotions. Preserve original versions when migrating the archive.

**Why:** The user requested all passages in NKJV and explicitly confirmed that previously published devotions are included.

**How to apply:** Replace actual quotations using a verified NKJV source, not translation labels alone. Preserve dates, titles, and original revision records. Confirm that source access permits the site's advertising-supported use before integrating a licensed provider.

Use available JCTM preaching materials as the foundation for ministry-specific devotion writing, and distinguish actual transcripts from descriptions or teaching notes.

**Why:** The user asked for content based on JCTM's preaching and explicitly prohibited invented minister quotations.

**How to apply:** Record the actual inspiration source for a reading. When only a sermon description or doctrinal note is available, label it accordingly rather than claiming to have heard or transcribed the sermon.
